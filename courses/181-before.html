Michigan LARA Hour Requirements

👆 New Applicants (This Course):

Required to complete 2 hours of implicit bias training within the 5 years immediately preceding issuance of license or registration. This course meets this requirement exactly.

🔄 Renewing Licensees:

Required to complete 1 hour of implicit bias training for each year of their license or registration cycle. This 2-hour course covers multiple cycles.

📄 Reporting Requirements:

You do not need to report your certificate to Michigan. During application or renewal, you'll attest to compliance. Keep your certificate for potential audit documentation. Important: This course is jointly provided with AKH CME. After completing the course and receiving your Renew Now CE certificate, you'll need to complete a brief evaluation on AKH's website to receive your joint provider certificate. This requires creating a free AKH account (one-time setup, takes about 2 minutes).
