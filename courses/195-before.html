Frequently Asked Questions about Renew Now CE's Complete Florida RN and LPN License Renewal Package

1. What exactly does this Florida CE package include?
This package includes all 27 hours required for Florida RN and LPN license renewal, including all mandatory state-required courses and general hours. Certificates are provided instantly after completion.

2. Is this package approved for Florida nurses?
Yes. All courses in this package are approved for Florida Registered Nurses (RN) and Licensed Practical Nurses (LPN) and meet Florida Board of Nursing renewal requirements.

3. Will my CE hours be reported to CE Broker automatically?
Yes. We report completed courses to CE Broker on your behalf. You do not need to upload certificates manually.

4. How soon will my hours appear in CE Broker?
CE hours are reported to CE Broker within 24 hours of course completion. In many cases, reporting happens sooner.

5. Do I need a paid CE Broker account?
No. A free CE Broker Basics account is sufficient to view your reported hours and confirm compliance.

6. Who should take this package?
This package is designed specifically for Florida RNs and LPNs who want a fast, complete, and stress-free way to meet all renewal requirements.

7. Does this package include APRN prescribing or controlled substance CE?
No. APRNs who need Safe & Effective Prescribing or controlled substance CE must complete those courses separately.

8. Are tests required to earn CE credit?
Yes. Florida requires a post-test for CE credit. Each course in this package includes a required test.

9. When do I need to complete my Florida CE?
CE must be completed before your Florida license renewal deadline (typically April 30 of your renewal year). Always confirm your personal deadline in CE Broker or your license portal.

10. Will I receive certificates after completing the courses?
Yes. Certificates are available immediately after successful completion and are stored in your account for future access.
