📜 Illinois Legal Authority

225 ILCS 2105-15.7 – Implicit Bias Awareness Training

"For license or registration renewals occurring on or after January 1, 2023, a health care professional who has continuing education requirements must complete at least a one-hour course in training on implicit bias awareness per renewal period."

The law further states that this training may count toward:

  • Minimum CE credit hours required for license renewal
  • Any other state licensure requirement
  • Professional accreditation or certification requirements
  • Healthcare institutional practice agreements

One course can satisfy multiple requirements – check with your employer if they require implicit bias training separately.
