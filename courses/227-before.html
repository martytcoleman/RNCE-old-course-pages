Illinois law requires RNs to complete specific CE topics each renewal cycle. This package covers all four:

1. Sexual Harassment Prevention Training

Required under 68 Ill. Adm. Code 1130.400 – Training on identifying, preventing, and responding to sexual harassment in healthcare environments.

1 Hour Required • ANCC P0557

2. Implicit Bias Awareness Training

Required per 225 ILCS 2105-15.7 – Training on recognizing and addressing unconscious biases in patient care. Effective January 1, 2023.

1 Hour Required • ANCC P0557

3. Cultural Competency Training

Required under 68 Ill. Adm. Code 1130.5250 – Skills for providing culturally sensitive care across diverse patient populations.

1 Hour Required • ANCC P0557

4. Alzheimer's Disease and Dementia Training

Required per 225 ILCS 2105-365 – Specialized training for nurses working with adults age 26 or older.

1 Hour Required* • ANCC P0557
