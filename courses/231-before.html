📋 California LVN CE Requirements at a Glance

Per 16 CCR § 2540.1, California Licensed Vocational Nurses must complete 30 contact hours of continuing education every two years to renew an active license. This package fulfills 100% of your renewal requirements.

Hours Required 30 contact hours every 2 years
First Renewal Exempt per 16 CCR § 2540.1(b)
Renewal Deadline Last day of your birth month (biannually)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does this package meet all California LVN CE requirements?

Yes. This package provides all 30 required CE hours for California LVN license renewal per 16 CCR § 2540.1. All courses are from BVNPT-approved provider VNCEP15132 and meet scope-of-practice requirements under California Business and Professions Code § 2859.

Is this my first renewal?

First-time renewers (first renewal after initial licensure) are exempt from the 30-hour CE requirement per 16 CCR § 2540.1(b). However, if your license has been delinquent for more than 24 months, CE is required.

What if I'm audited by the BVNPT?

Our completion certificates include all documentation required under 16 CCR § 2540.5 for BVNPT audits: completion date, provider number, course title, and total hours. Retain certificates for the required 4-year period per 16 CCR § 2540.5(c).

Can I complete this on my mobile device?

Absolutely. Our courses are fully mobile-optimized for learning on smartphones and tablets. Progress tracking lets you start, pause, and resume courses anytime.

What topics count toward my CE hours?

Per 16 CCR § 2540.2, continuing education must be relevant to LVN scope of practice. This includes pharmacology, patient care, clinical procedures, and healthcare topics applicable to vocational nursing.

How long do I have to complete the courses?

You have 24/7 access to complete courses at your own pace. All courses must be completed within two years prior to your license renewal date per BVNPT requirements.
