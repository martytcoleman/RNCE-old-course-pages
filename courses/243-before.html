Important renewal note

This package includes 27 total contact hours. Some included topics, such as HIV/AIDS and Domestic Violence, may apply based on renewal cycle or license status. Complete the courses that apply to your renewal and keep certificates for your records.

Florida Required Nursing CE Topics Included

Human Trafficking Prevention - 2 Hours

Required for Florida nurses. Covers identification and response considerations for human trafficking in healthcare settings.

Prevention of Medical Errors - 2 Hours

Required every renewal. Covers patient safety and evidence-based strategies for reducing medical errors.

Florida Laws and Rules - 2 Hours

Required every renewal. Reviews Florida nursing practice regulations and professional responsibilities.

Recognizing Impairment in the Workplace - 2 Hours

Required every renewal. Covers recognizing and responding to impairment in healthcare workplace settings.

HIV/AIDS Education - 1 Hour

Included for nurses who need the HIV/AIDS education requirement, commonly associated with first renewal or applicable license status.

Domestic Violence Prevention - 2 Hours

Included for nurses whose renewal cycle requires domestic violence CE. This topic may apply every third renewal.

General CE Courses Included
