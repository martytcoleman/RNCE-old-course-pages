Michigan Nursing CE Requirements

Michigan nurses must complete 25 hours of continuing education within the two-year renewal period. At least two of these hours must be in pain and symptom management.

Michigan allows a maximum of 12 CE hours in a 24-hour period, which is why this package includes four hours of essential CE courses.

Nurses with specialty licenses may have additional CE requirements. Please refer to the Administrative Rules for Health Professionals for more details. Click here to review Michigan Board of Nursing requirements.
