This 30-hour continuing education package is intended for California Registered Nurses renewing their license for the second or subsequent time.

Under California Board of Registered Nursing (BRN) regulations, RNs renewing an active license after the first renewal cycle are required to complete 30 contact hours of continuing education during each two-year renewal period.

If this is your FIRST California RN license renewal, you are exempt from the 30-hour continuing education requirement and are instead required to complete only the Board-mandated 1-hour Implicit Bias training.

This requirement is outlined by the California Board of Registered Nursing pursuant to California Code of Regulations, Title 16, Section 1450, and the BRN’s continuing education renewal guidance.
