📜 Illinois Legal Authority & Provider Qualification

68 Ill. Adm. Code 1130.400 – Sexual Harassment Prevention Training

All persons who hold a professional license through IDFPR must complete 1 hour of sexual harassment prevention training during each renewal cycle.

Why Renew Now CE Can Serve Multiple Professions:

As an ANCC-accredited provider (P0557), Renew Now CE is recognized as a Continuing Education Sponsor by the Illinois Board of Nursing, administered by IDFPR. Per Illinois regulation:

"An entity that is recognized as a continuing education provider under any licensing Act administered by the Division or its rules for any profession licensed by the Division" — 68 Ill. Adm. Code 1130.400(b)(2)

This allows us to provide this required CE course to all IDFPR-licensed professions subject to the sexual harassment prevention training requirement. Your completion certificate is valid for all applicable licenses you hold.
