About This Critical Pharmacology Training

This nationally accredited course offers 6 comprehensive pharmacology hours approved for APRN license renewal and certification. As prescription drug misuse continues to be a significant public health concern, understanding medications with potential for dependency is essential for safe, responsible prescribing practices.

The course explores commonly prescribed medications with potential for misuse or dependency, outlines current regulatory standards for controlled substances, and reviews safe disposal practices for healthcare settings. Updated in June 2024, this content reflects the latest regulatory guidelines and evidence-based practices.

🎯 What You'll Learn: Essential Learning Objectives

  1. Medication Identification: Identify medications with potential for misuse or dependency, including commonly prescribed substances across therapeutic categories and their abuse potential profiles.
  2. Regulatory Standards: Describe current regulations and prescribing guidelines for controlled substances, including DEA requirements and state-specific protocols.
  3. Safe Disposal Practices: Identify appropriate resources and best practices for the safe disposal of controlled substances in healthcare settings to prevent diversion and environmental harm.
