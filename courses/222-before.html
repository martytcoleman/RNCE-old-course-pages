Healthcare Professionals Required to Complete Alzheimer's Training in Illinois

Illinois Public Act 102-0399 applies to the following healthcare professionals:

Medical Professionals:

  • Physician (medical & osteopathic)
  • Physician Assistant
  • Chiropractic Physician
  • Podiatric Physician
  • Optometrist

Nursing Professionals:

  • Advanced Practice Registered Nurse (APRN)
  • Registered Nurse (RN)
  • Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN)
  • Nursing Home Administrator

Allied Health Professionals:

  • Physical Therapist
  • Physical Therapist Assistant
  • Occupational Therapist
  • Occupational Therapy Assistant
  • Respiratory Care Practitioner

Mental Health Professionals:

  • Clinical Psychologist
  • Clinical Social Worker
  • Social Worker
  • Clinical Professional Counselor
  • Professional Counselor

Communication Specialists:

  • Speech-Language Pathologist
  • Speech-Language Pathology Assistant
  • Audiologist

Important: This requirement typically applies to professionals who work with adults age 26 or older in their practice. Check with your specific licensing board to confirm applicability.

Sec. 2105-365. Continuing education; Alzheimer's disease and other dementias
Any training on Alzheimer's disease and other dementias applied to meet any other State licensure requirement, professional accreditation or certification requirement, or health care institutional practice agreement may count toward the continuing education requirement under this Section.
