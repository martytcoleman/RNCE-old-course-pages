❓ Frequently Asked Questions: Pain Management CE Requirements by State

What are the Michigan pain management CE requirements for nurses?

Michigan requires all RNs and LPNs to complete 2 hours of pain and pain symptom management CE as part of their 25-hour renewal requirement. This is mandated by the Michigan Board of Nursing under LARA regulations. This course satisfies the full Michigan pain management requirement and provides pharmacology credit.

What is the Wisconsin controlled substance CE requirement for nurses?

The Wisconsin Board of Nursing requires nurses to complete 2 contact hours in responsible prescribing of controlled substances for license renewal. This course fulfills the complete Wisconsin controlled substance requirement and covers opioid prescribing, abuse prevention, and proper pain management protocols.

What are the Massachusetts MCSR requirements for APRNs?

Massachusetts requires APRNs renewing their Massachusetts Controlled Substance Registration (MCSR) to complete training in pain management and controlled substance prescribing. This course satisfies the Massachusetts MCSR and APRN license renewal requirements for controlled substance education.

What is the Alaska opioid CE requirement for Nurse Practitioners?

Alaska requires Nurse Practitioners who hold a valid federal DEA registration number to complete 2 hours in pain management and opioid use and addiction per renewal cycle. This course satisfies the complete Alaska NP opioid education requirement.

What is the Delaware substance abuse training requirement for Nurse Practitioners?

Delaware requires Nurse Practitioners to complete 2 hours of substance abuse training for license renewal. This course covers controlled substance regulation, opioid abuse prevention, and responsible prescribing practices to satisfy the Delaware requirement.

What is the Indiana opioid prescribing CE requirement?

Indiana requires Nurse Practitioners to complete 2 hours addressing opioid prescribing and abuse as part of their continuing education requirements. This course covers opioid classification, prescribing guidelines, abuse potential, and overdose prevention to meet Indiana's mandate.

Does this course count toward my total CE hours for license renewal?

Yes. This 2-hour course counts toward your total CE requirement in addition to satisfying state-specific pain management mandates. For example, in Michigan, these 2 hours count toward your 25-hour total AND fulfill the mandatory pain management requirement.

Is this course ANCC accredited?

Yes. Renew Now CE is accredited by the American Nurses Credentialing Center (ANCC) as Provider P0557. This course provides 2 contact hours of pharmacology CE that meets national accreditation standards and is accepted across all 50 states for general nursing CE.
