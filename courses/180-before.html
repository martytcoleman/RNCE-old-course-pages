📋 California Implicit Bias CE Requirements

AB 1407 - New California Nurse Licensees:

All newly licensed California RNs and LVNs must complete 1 hour of implicit bias training within their first two years of licensure. This requirement applies to nurses licensed after January 1, 2023.

AB 2319 - Perinatal Care Providers:

  • New perinatal providers: Complete implicit bias training within six months of start date
  • Who must comply: All California healthcare providers involved in perinatal care including labor and delivery nurses, postpartum nurses, NICU nurses, and certified nurse-midwives
  • Facility obligation: Healthcare facilities must provide or accept training certificates from approved providers
✓ This course satisfies both AB 1407 and AB 2319 requirements for California RNs, LVNs, APRNs, and other healthcare providers.
