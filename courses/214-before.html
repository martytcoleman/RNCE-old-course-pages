IMPLICIT BIAS TRAINING – 1 HOUR (INTERPROFESSIONAL)

This comprehensive 1-hour interprofessional course equips healthcare providers with a deep understanding of implicit bias and its impacts on patient care. Through real-world case examples and evidence-based practices, you'll explore how biases—especially related to race, gender, and socioeconomic status—can uniquely affect individuals with intersecting identities. You'll gain practical tools and actionable strategies to effectively identify, interrupt, and mitigate bias in your daily clinical interactions. This course specifically meets mandatory implicit bias training standards in Illinois (Section 1130.500), California (AB 2319), and New Jersey (Bill A1079).

Renew Now CE is jointly accredited through AKH Inc., Advancing Knowledge in Healthcare by the Accreditation Council for Continuing Medical Education (ACCME), the Accreditation Council for Pharmacy Education (ACPE), and the American Nurses Credentialing Center (ANCC), to provide continuing education for the healthcare team, ensuring your compliance with state requirements.

Learning Objectives:

  • Clearly differentiate implicit bias from explicit bias, and describe how each impacts individuals with intersecting identities.
  • Recognize and describe the root causes, formation processes, harmful effects, and broader impacts of implicit bias related to race, gender, socioeconomic status, and intersecting identities.
  • Apply proven strategies to identify, interrupt, and mitigate implicit biases effectively during clinical interactions with diverse patient populations.

Course Content Requirements: This implicit bias training covers all required elements, including:

  • Identification of unconscious biases and misinformation.
  • Examination of personal, interpersonal, institutional, structural, and cultural barriers.
  • Strategies for corrective measures at individual and organizational levels.
  • Exploration of historical and contemporary impacts of exclusion on minority communities.
  • Effective communication techniques across diverse identities.
  • Power dynamics and organizational decision-making processes.
  • Specific discussions on health inequities in perinatal care and reproductive justice.
  • Inclusion of diverse local perspectives on racial, cultural, and community-provider relationships.

Target Audience:

This 1-hour course is ideal for an interprofessional audience nationwide, including:

  • All healthcare professionals licensed or registered under the Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation as per Section 1130.500.
  • Professionals licensed under Division 2 of California's Business and Professions Code who provide perinatal care and require compliance with AB 2319.
  • Physicians, physician assistants, nurses, certified nurse midwives, certified professional midwives, and certified midwives in New Jersey providing prenatal or perinatal care as mandated by Bill A1079.
